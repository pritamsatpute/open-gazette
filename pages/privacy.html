Information we collect

Open Gazette may collect information that you voluntarily provide when interacting with our website. This may include your name, email address or other information submitted through forms such as newsletter subscriptions or contact forms.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information about how visitors use the website, including browser type, device information, pages visited and general usage data.

How we use information

Information collected through Open Gazette may be used to operate and improve the website, respond to enquiries, deliver requested communications and better understand how visitors interact with our content.

We aim to use information only for legitimate purposes related to operating and improving Open Gazette.

Cookies and similar technologies

Open Gazette may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences and understand general usage patterns.

You can usually manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how some parts of the website function.

Third-party services

Open Gazette may use third-party services to support features such as analytics, advertising, email delivery, embedded content or other website functionality.

These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the relevant policies when interacting with third-party services through our website.

Data protection

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights regarding the personal information held about you. These rights may include requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

If you would like to make a request regarding your information, you can contact us using the details provided below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Open Gazette, legal requirements or the services and technologies we use.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised last updated date.
